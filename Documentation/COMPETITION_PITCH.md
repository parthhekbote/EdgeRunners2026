# Competition Pitch

**Working title:** Cyberpunk Dev
**Tagline:** *Rebuilt from scrap. Built to burn it all down.*

## The pitch

She was built for war and rebuilt from scrap, and the one thing that made her a weapon is missing: her antimatter heart. Play Alita across three worlds: a neon city that hates her, a rogue AI's cyberspace and a black market that sells her out. Then fight your way to Mars to take your heart back from a war machine the size of a building.

## Why it stands out

1. **It plays like a film.** Cutscenes are directed shot by shot and flow straight into play. There is no cut to a menu or a loading screen between the story and the fight.
2. **Every chapter is a new game.** Street combat, a chase, a gladiator ladder, a duel boss, a cyberspace siege, a heist with starship flight and a tractor beam, and a Titan boss.
3. **Bosses that read you.** The Titan watches how you move. Run away and it leaps on you, circle it and it cuts you off, stand still and it punishes you. Its biggest attacks are always shown first, so every defeat is the player's mistake, never the game's.
4. **A world with a voice.** Every dialogue box is spoken, each main character has a distinct voice, and the music shifts with the moment.

## Judging sheet

**Concept**
A one-woman war against the corporations that built her, told as a hunt for a stolen heart. Simple to say, easy to feel.

**Visual**
Stylised neon sci-fi lit like a film: rain on the scrapyard, glowing rings in a 56 m arena, a 200 m cyberspace barrier that pulses with the music, a red Martian dust storm under Phobos and a fleet of dreadnoughts. Hits freeze for a beat, swords leave glowing trails and boss attacks are marked by glowing rings on the ground.

**Story / Plot**
A scrapyard rebirth, a lunar-war flashback, a mentor lost, a rogue AI with the truth, a trusted broker who betrays her and a final fight on Mars. Loss, betrayal and identity drive every chapter.

**Audio**
A different track for each chapter, with crossfades as the action changes: a chase theme, an industrial cyberspace fight, drums and a roaring crowd in the arena, and a Mars build-up that rolls into the boss theme. Sound slows down in bullet time, a perfect parry freezes time for a beat, and every voiced line has its own character.

**Gameplay**
Fast sword-and-gun combat: a 7-hit combo, heavy strikes, a gun burst, a dodge dash, block and perfect parry, bullet time and lock-on. Enemies have poise and attack in coordinated groups, and bosses have clear phases. Around the fighting there is a loot-and-money inventory, a living market full of talkers, and a flyable starship.

**Technical**
Built in Unity 6 around a set of custom systems: directed cinematics driven by code, adaptive boss AI, a voice pipeline for the dialogue, and tools that rebuild scenes. About 25 scenes.

## What to demo (3 minutes)

1. **0:00** The scrapyard wake-up cinematic.

3. **1:20** The Pit and the Vulcan boss.
